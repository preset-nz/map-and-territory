## 0.1.0 (2026-11-04)

The first public build. A hexmap editor for tabletop RPGs: paint terrain from a setting, ink a hex grid, add a title, a compass and locations, and export a PNG or a printable PDF zine page.

### Features

- A campaign holds maps; each map is a stack of layers: paper, freeform terrain, hex noise, outline, image, locations, title, compass and the hex grid.
- Nine built-in settings colour the terrain; a map can follow the campaign's or pick its own.
- Paint, Erase and Fill tools, with Undo and Redo for every edit.
- Export the active map as PNG, or the campaign's pages as a PDF.
- A native menu bar; the window comes back where it was.
